Frequently asked questions

Simple answers to the questions we hear most often.

These questions help you understand quickly how we work and how we can start.

01 Do you work with small and mid-sized businesses?

Yes. We work with growing teams and with companies that need fuller support.

02 Do you only handle IT, or also automation and AI?

Yes. We connect IT, automation, reporting, security, and AI when that makes work easier.

03 How does collaboration start?

Usually with a short conversation, a look at the current situation, and a clear proposal for the next steps.

04 Can we work in phases?

Yes. We can start with one problem, one package, or one area, then expand step by step.

05 How long does a typical implementation take?

It depends on the area and complexity. A website may move faster, while an internal system, a full audit, or a security implementation requires analysis, testing, and a more controlled delivery.

06 Can you take over and improve an existing system?

Yes. We often improve existing systems: we check them, clean them up, document them, and move them into a clearer standard.

07 Do you provide maintenance and support after delivery?

Yes. After delivery we can continue with monitoring, updates, security checks, documentation, and support.

08 How do you handle security and access control?

Security is planned from the beginning: roles, access, policies, backups, activity logs, and recovery planning.

09 Do you work remotely, on site, or both?

We work remotely, on site, or with a mix, depending on what the project needs.

10 Can we start with an audit before a larger project?

Yes. An initial audit helps us understand the real situation, the risks, and the priorities before implementation.

11 Do you adapt the solution to our budget and phase of growth?

Yes. We shape the scope around the company's priorities, budget, and current stage.

12 What happens after delivery?

After delivery, we can continue with maintenance, monitoring, support, and updates so the system stays stable.
