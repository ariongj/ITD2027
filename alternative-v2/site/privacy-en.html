Who we are

The controller of the data for this website is:

IT Department, based in Prishtina, Kosovo · info@itdks.tech · +383 49 573 570

This policy applies to itdks.tech and to the enquiries you send us through it. The legal framework is Law No. 06/L-082 on the Protection of Personal Data.

Data, purposes and retention

We process only the data needed for each purpose. For each flow:

Enquiry form

Data
Name, email, company and phone if provided, the selected service, your message, and the form type and language.
Purpose
To reply and, if you ask for it, to prepare an offer.
Legal basis
Steps taken at your request before a contract; our legitimate interest in replying.
Retention
The enquiry passes through Formspree and reaches our inbox. We keep it as long as needed for the enquiry and any collaboration that follows; longer only where the law requires it, for example for contracts and invoices.

Draft in your browser

Data
The text you type into the form, before sending.
Purpose
So your text is not lost if you reload the page or switch language.
Legal basis
Legitimate interest in a working form; the draft is not sent to us until you submit it.
Retention
Only in this browser tab (sessionStorage), until the enquiry is sent successfully or the tab is closed.

Email, phone and WhatsApp

Data
Your email address or number and the content of your message.
Purpose
Communicating about your enquiry.
Legal basis
Steps before a contract, at your request; legitimate interest in replying.
Retention
As for form enquiries. WhatsApp opens only when you click the link and operates under its own terms.

Booking a meeting

Data
Name, email, the selected time and the answers you give in Calendly.
Purpose
Organising the meeting.
Legal basis
Steps before a contract, at your request.
Retention
Calendly loads only when you open the booking and keeps the data under its own terms; we use it as long as needed for the meeting and any collaboration that follows.

Technical server data

Data
IP address, date and time, the requested address, the referring page and the browser type.
Purpose
Delivering the site, operating it and protecting it against misuse.
Legal basis
Our legitimate interest in a secure, working website.
Retention
For a limited time, according to the settings of the hosting provider (Hostinger).

Map and fonts

Data
When you click “Show map”, Google Maps receives your IP address and technical device data.
Purpose
Showing the approximate location. The font (Outfit) is served by this website, with no connection to Google Fonts.
Legal basis
Your request by clicking; the map does not load before you click.
Retention
Under Google’s terms.

In this version of the site Google Analytics is not active and no measurement cookies are set.

Please do not send passwords or sensitive data, such as health information, through the form or messages.

External services

For some functions we use external providers. Each receives only the data its service needs and has its own privacy policy:

  • Hostinger: website hosting
  • Formspree: form delivery
  • Google: Maps only after a click
  • Calendly: booking meetings, when you open it
  • WhatsApp: conversations, when you click the link

The social networks at the bottom of the page open only when you click them. Some of these providers process data outside Kosovo, including in the European Union and the United States, under their own terms and safeguards.

Your rights

Under Law No. 06/L-082 you can request access to your data, its correction or deletion, restriction of processing and, where applicable, data portability, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest.

To exercise these rights, write to info@itdks.tech. If you believe your rights have been infringed, you can lodge a complaint with the Information and Privacy Agency (AIP).

Cookies and measurement
