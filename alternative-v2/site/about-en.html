FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Working together

Do you work with small and mid-sized businesses?+
Yes. We work with growing teams and with companies that need fuller support.
How does collaboration start?+
Usually with a short conversation, a look at the current situation, and a clear proposal for the next steps.
Do you work remotely, on site, or both?+
We work remotely, on site, or with a mix, depending on what the project needs.
Do you adapt the solution to our budget and phase of growth?+
Yes. We shape the scope around the company's priorities, budget, and current stage.

Services

Do you provide IT services only, or automation and AI as well?+
Alongside IT services, we offer automation and AI where they fit the project. We connect them with reporting, security and team workflows.
Can you take over and improve an existing system?+
Yes. We often improve existing systems: we check them, clean them up, document them, and move them into a clearer standard.
Can we start with an audit before a larger project?+
Yes. An initial audit helps us understand the current setup, risks and priorities before implementation.

Process

Can we work in phases?+
Yes. We can start with one problem, one package, or one area, then expand step by step.
How long does a typical implementation take?+
The timeline depends on scope and complexity. We agree it after discovery, including testing and handover stages.

Support

Do you provide maintenance and support after delivery?+
Yes. After delivery we can continue with monitoring, updates, security checks, documentation, and support.
How do you handle security and access control?+
Security is planned from the beginning: roles, access, policies, backups, activity logs, and recovery planning.
What happens after delivery?+
After delivery, we can continue with maintenance, monitoring, support, and updates so the system stays stable.
