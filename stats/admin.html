What gets recorded

With the visitor's consent: page views, language, page type, referring website, UTM source and campaign, phone, email and WhatsApp clicks, confirmed bookings, demo requests and confirmed enquiries. No names, emails, phone numbers or messages.

Important: the IP address and the random browser identifier are stored only as keyed hashes. Country uses hosting headers when available, with the browser language as a fallback. Events older than 90 days are removed.
