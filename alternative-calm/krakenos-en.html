What it is

Your whole business in one shared workspace.

Kraken OS is a modular platform for running a business. It brings client relationships, sales, projects, tasks, documents, finance, the team and resources together in one place.

The client, the document, the person responsible and the next step stay in the same context. Teams work with the modules they need, while management keeps the overall view.

A business can start with a few modules and expand its use as its needs grow.

How it is organised

Three layers, one platform.

01

Business modules

Clients, sales, work, finance, people and resources.

02

Shared functions

Documents, search, communication, notifications, reports and AURA.

03

Administration

Users, roles, configuration, audit trail, privacy and operational continuity.

From client to report

How the modules connect.

  1. Client
  2. Sales opportunity
  3. Offer
  4. Project
  5. Tasks
  6. Invoice
  7. Payment
  8. Report

The contact is created in the CRM, the offer is prepared and approved, the work is delivered as a project, the invoice is linked to the delivery, payments are recorded and the report is analysed with AURA.
